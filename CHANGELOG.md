## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-10

- Encode, decode and inspect Cardano bech32 addresses (BIP-173, CIP-19, CIP-5 prefixes).
- Address builders and credential/network inspection for Shelley address types.
- Typed errors and non-throwing `try*` / `is*` helpers.
- Zero runtime dependencies; works in Node.js and browsers (`Uint8Array` only).
